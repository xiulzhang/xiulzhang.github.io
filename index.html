Welcome to my website! I am a Ph.D. candidate in Government and Politics at the University of Maryland, College Park.

I study comparative political behavior and the political economy of development in Sub-Saharan Africa, with a focus on the political consequences of identity biases. My dissertation investigates how patrilineal ethnic systems interact with gender to create distinct structural challenges for women’s political representation. Methodologically, my work integrates causal inference, computational tools, and qualitative field research.

I hold an M.A. in African Studies from UCLA and a dual B.A. in International Politics and Economics from Peking University.

I am on the 2026-27 academic job market.
